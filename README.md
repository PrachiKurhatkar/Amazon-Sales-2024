## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents a **dynamic** and **AI-driven** **Sales Dashboard** built using **Excel & Power BI**, offering deep insights into sales, revenue, and customer trends.

### 🔹 Key Highlights:
- **📊 Sales Performance Analysis** – Track revenue, sales trends & target achievements  
- **📍 City & State-Wise Sales Insights** – Interactive **heatmaps & charts**  
- **📦 Product-Wise Sales Breakdown** – Identify **top-performing products**  
- **🎯 KPI Metrics** – Sales, Units Sold, and Market Reach  
- **📊 Data Slicers & Filters** – Enhance **interactivity & usability**
